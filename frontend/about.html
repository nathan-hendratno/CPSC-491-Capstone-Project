About Credible Sorcerer

Meet Our Team: - Nathan Hendratno = Team Lead and Project Designer - Liyuhan Zhou = AI Script Developer - Jake Mendez = Article & File Extraction Developer - Seamus Flanagan = Scoring Engine Developer

Credible Sorcerer is an AI-powered system designed to evaluate the credibility of online articles and PDF documents. It analyzes metadata, authorship, citations, and AI-generated content signals to produce a comprehensive credibility score.

This project was developed for our CPSC 491 Undergraduate Capstone Project. It is our academic endevor to explore the intersection of artificial intelligence, information reliability, and user-facing web applications.

Note: This tool provides guidance and should not replace critical thinking or verification from trusted sources.
